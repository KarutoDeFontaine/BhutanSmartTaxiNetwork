[Platform Tutorial & User Guide]

Step-by-Step Explanation for New Users

[How Passenger Booking Works]
  1. Step 1: Select your Pickup Station and Destination Station from the dropdown menu.
  2. Step 2: Review the calculated fare summary which factors distance and total passengers.
  3. Step 3: Click "Dispatch Cab Call" to broadcast your booking to drivers in Gelephu.
  4. Step 4: Once a driver accepts, confirm their profile to initiate live GPS tracking and start the trip.
  5. Step 5: Click "In-App Chat" to send direct text messages to your driver.
[How Driver Terminal Works]
  1. Step 1: Log in as a Driver to view the live dispatch queue and LED Matrix Terminal.
  2. Step 2: When a new passenger call appears, click "Accept Call".
  3. Step 3: Wait for passenger confirmation, then follow the live turn-by-turn route.
  4. Step 4: Communicate directly via the encrypted chat modal without leaving the app.
